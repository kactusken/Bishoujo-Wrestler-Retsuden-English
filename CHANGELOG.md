# Changelog

## v0.1
First public release.
- All text in English: the 3,413 story lines of Champion Road and the World Circuit, the
  wrestlers' names and profiles, menus, schedule and training help, move names, match commentary
  and winners' quotes.
- A new 8×16 English font for the story and menus; names packed two letters to a tile in the
  game's name font.
- Screen layout fixes for the longer English: a wider selection bar, a larger dialogue buffer,
  moved labels and headers.
- Left in Japanese on purpose: the title logo, the menu background, the save screen's ticket
  picture and the title screen's copyright line.
